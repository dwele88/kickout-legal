Privacy Policy

Kickout 246 Live — last updated October 5, 2026.

Data we collect

Our TikTok developer app collects the minimum needed to function:

  • Account identity (via TikTok Login Kit): only to verify the connected account is @kickout246live.
  • Uploaded videos: clips we create ourselves, uploaded to our own account's drafts via TikTok's Content Posting API.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not collect, store, or process personal data of TikTok viewers, followers, or any third party.
  • We do not use cookies, trackers, or analytics.

How data is used

Account identity is used only at login to confirm the right account. Uploaded videos are used only to stage our own posts.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. The only data transfer is the one you authorize: your videos to your own TikTok drafts through TikTok's official API.

Data retention

We keep no user database. OAuth tokens are stored securely and only for maintaining the connection you approved; revoke access any time from your TikTok settings.

Contact

Privacy questions: DM @kickout246live on TikTok or Instagram.
